If you need help with Resrv, you can reach out to me:

## Pricing

Resrv is not free. You can use it for free during development, but you'll need to [purchase a license]({{marketplaceUrl}}) before going live.

The {{license}} costs {{price}}, and here's what it covers:

- **One live site.** A license covers one production site and as many development sites as you need. Statamic treats a site's domains as wildcards, so a subdomain of your live domain, such as a staging site, is covered too. Each other live site needs its own license.
- **Paid once.** It's a one-time purchase with no annual renewal.
- **Every update.** All future releases, major versions included, come with your license at no extra cost.
- **No commission.** Resrv takes nothing from your bookings.

Given the work that's gone into it and the types of websites it's designed for, I believe the price is fair. However, if you're unable to afford it or are using Resrv for a non-profit or open-source project, feel free to reach out to me.
